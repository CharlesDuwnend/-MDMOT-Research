# Next direction audit after P45

## Result of the two proposed pivots

1. **P45 relation-to-plan module:** stopped by direct graph-matching collision. See `p45_causal_relational_transport/DECISION.json`.
2. **Latent cross-view time-offset module:** not admissible on the current MDMT evidence. The pinned event schema and field audit show `timestamp`, `delay`, and `synchronization_flag` unavailable/null; the paired protocol uses shared frame IDs and constant image/XML frame offsets. A learned lag posterior would therefore invent an unobserved nuisance factor and cannot be claimed as a data-grounded MDMT contribution.

## What remains defensible

The strongest paper route is still the train-only MDMT-COD protocol/diagnosis: expose the gap between candidate generation and identity ownership under the frozen MIA host, with formal candidate recall, given-candidate ranking, persistent false commits, and a legal prefix-causal replay. It is a protocol/diagnostic contribution, not a new SOTA association module.

An algorithmic route is not authorized until a new observable and learning target survive a direct same-task audit. The next audit must start from an unrepresented failure signal in the detector/tracker outputs, not from another name for temporal fusion, graph matching, ReID, quality gating, or delayed owner revision.
