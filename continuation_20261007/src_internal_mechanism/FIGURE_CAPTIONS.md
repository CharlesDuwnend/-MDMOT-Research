# SRC internal-mechanism figure captions

**Cost selectivity.** Empirical responses of the SRC cost operator on frozen calibration association states. (a) Recorded masked geometry/appearance base cost C. (b) The same candidate edges after modulation by C_tilde = C + (1-C)u, using the current frozen SRC heads, population prior and causally reconstructed semantic beliefs. All eight calibration sequences are included. Only first-stage edges with C <= 0.80 and both endpoints labeled as known foreground are evaluated. Labels use unique class-agnostic GT geometry matches; a track's owner refers to its last committed observation. Solid, dashed and dotted curves represent same-owner edges (n = 191,731), different-owner edges within one coarse group (n = 18,330), and different-owner edges across coarse groups (n = 23,950), respectively. Groups are human and vehicle. At the unchanged threshold, 99.5% of same-owner edges remain eligible and 98.2% of cross-group mismatches become ineligible. Each candidate edge contributes once to the empirical CDF. Costs are recomputed on recorded H2 trajectories; no changed assignment is executed. These are internal development diagnostics, not formal tracking improvements or independent-sample significance tests.

**Semantic evidence reliability.** Learned SRC observation probabilities compared with the predeclared raw coarse-group confidence construction on 26,088 fixed, GT-free-thinned calibration observations from eight sequences. Raw confidence assigns the reported confidence to the predicted coarse group and splits residual mass uniformly. Ten fixed bins cover [0,1]; every nonempty bin is shown. The x coordinate is mean predicted human-group probability and the y coordinate is empirical human-group frequency. Marker area is 5 + 0.35 sqrt(n), so sparse bins remain visible. The dashed diagonal indicates perfect empirical calibration. The frozen learned head reduces NLL from 0.108 to 0.038 and two-class Brier score from 0.032 to 0.018. Fit and calibration flights are disjoint. These descriptive probability diagnostics establish input reliability; they do not claim a separate temporal-update or tracking-performance gain.
