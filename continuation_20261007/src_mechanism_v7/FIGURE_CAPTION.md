SRC semantic evidence and cost refinement. (a) Illustrative class-level semantic evidence over car, van, truck, pedestrian and other, showing contrasting historical track and incoming detection preferences. Blue and golden-orange bars denote the track and detection, respectively. These specified five-class values explain the semantic-evidence concept; the implementation stores beliefs over human and vehicle, and the illustrated distributions are not the numerical inputs to (b,c). (b) Analytic disagreement responses as observation probability varies, using the saved learned prior from the real case in (c). H-track and V-track curves use b=(1,0) and b=(0,1); prior-like belief b=π produces zero disagreement. Faint curves interpolate each limiting belief halfway toward the prior, illustrating attenuated penalties from less certain beliefs; shaded regions span these belief-conditioned responses. Compatible observations lie on the zero-penalty segments, while increasingly contradictory evidence produces a graded penalty. Hollow circles locate the two conflicting pairs selected by the base solver in the real case. Reliability affects the post-commitment belief update and is not multiplied into these penalties. (c) The same closed candidate component before and after SRC cost refinement, with the fixed eligibility threshold of 0.80. Solid and dashed outlines identify selected same-owner and different-owner pairs; crosses mark costs above the threshold. The base component has two tied minimum-cost assignments, whereas SRC leaves one unique compatible optimum. Panel (a) is a specified illustration, (b) is an equation-based response, and (c) is real calibration-case evidence; the figure is not a population-level online performance result.
