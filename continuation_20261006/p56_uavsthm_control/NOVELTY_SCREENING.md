# P56 novelty boundary

`STOP_AS_NOVEL_METHOD`: UAVST-HM is a direct same-task MDMT prior. Any regional
appearance aggregation, adaptive fusion of appearance and geometry, or Hungarian
cross-view assignment in this phase is prior art. The only permitted purpose is
to establish a paper-comparable control and identify a measurable gap for a
future method.

The control is narrower than the paper because the local P23 identity vector is
used in place of the paper's FastReID implementation and the geometry/Hungarian
host is not yet ported. A positive readout therefore authorizes a faithful
implementation audit only; it does not authorize an innovation claim.
