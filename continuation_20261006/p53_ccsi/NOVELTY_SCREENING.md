# CCSI novelty and collision screen

No `first`, `novel`, or `SOTA` claim is authorized. The surrounding families
are established: temporal memory and graph association (MeMOT, ReST, TransMOT),
video ReID feature disentanglement and temporal aggregation, and general
counterfactual/disentangled representation learning. The CCFI result in this
workspace is also a direct local precursor.

The provisional narrow mechanism is different in its testable contract: a
strict-prefix same-view temporal-change swap is used as a counterfactual and the
cross-view identity core is required to be invariant to that swap. This is a
mechanism hypothesis, not a novelty conclusion. If it does not improve the
held-out pair gate without fit overfitting, it will be stopped.
